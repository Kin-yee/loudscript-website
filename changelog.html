Changelog

New features, improvements, and fixes in every public release.

47 releases in 2026 so far.

Version 0.1.46 Latest 13 November 2026 Show notes Hide notes

  • A new welcome flow helps you pick a voice, hear it before you download it, learn the read shortcut, and turn on selected-text reading.
  • AI Formatting is available again. Turn it on in Settings to tidy text before it is read aloud.
  • Text that mixes languages is now read paragraph by paragraph in each language, and a single foreign word no longer switches the voice.
  • Chinese and Japanese text in screenshots is now recognized, and screenshots and PDFs with several columns are read one column at a time.
  • Lines that wrap mid-sentence are now read as one sentence, and words hyphenated at a line or page break are read whole.
  • Imported PDFs no longer read running headers, page numbers, or footnote markers aloud.
  • Supertonic now reads long sentences, powers such as m², and fractions such as 1¾ correctly, and no longer stumbles over emoji.
  • Switching between Formatted and Original now plays the text on screen from the beginning.

Version 0.1.45 11 November 2026 Show notes Hide notes

  • New Phonon speech model: a compact English voice that reads numbers, prices, links, and abbreviations naturally. Requires macOS 15 or later.
  • PocketTTS is no longer in beta.
  • Setup now lets you hear a first read right away, without granting Screen Recording. LoudScript asks for it the first time it needs to read text from your screen.
  • Fixed brief freezes during playback, especially with Settings open.

Version 0.1.44 9 November 2026 Show notes Hide notes

  • A new Models tab in Settings lets you download, update, switch, and remove speech models.
  • Switch between installed speech models from the control bar or the Playback menu. Each model now has its own icon.
  • Playback speed now goes up to 2x, and the speaking overlay shows the current speed.
  • The Play/Pause shortcut now starts reading the selected text when nothing is playing.
  • When the selected speech model can't speak a text's language, LoudScript now reads it with another installed model or an Apple voice for that language instead of staying silent.
  • Speech starts sooner with the Supertonic model.
  • Cancel and Esc now stop a Read shortcut that is still capturing text from the screen.
  • Fixed brief freezes during playback, and improved reliability when an on-device voice fails mid-read.
