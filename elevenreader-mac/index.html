LoudScript / ElevenReader on Mac

Is there an ElevenReader app for Mac?

Not as a native Mac app. ElevenLabs says ElevenReader works on desktop through the web at elevenreader.io in all major browsers. Its App Store listing covers iPhone and iPad only. If you want a native Mac reader for selected text, screenshots and PDFs, LoudScript is free and runs its voices on your Mac.

How can you use ElevenReader on a Mac today?

  • In the browser. Sign in at elevenreader.io. ElevenLabs’ help center says it supports all major browsers.
  • With the Chrome extension. The ElevenReader site links to an extension in the Chrome Web Store.
  • On your iPhone or iPad. The free plan syncs your library between the web and mobile apps.

The App Store listing for ElevenReader names iPhone and iPad and lists no Mac requirements. The ElevenReader site links to iOS, Android and Chrome downloads, with no Mac download.

What does ElevenReader include?

  • A free plan with 10 hours of text to audio every month.
  • Ultra for $11 a month or $99 a year, with unlimited listening for your own imports and downloads for offline listening.
  • Imports of PDF, DOCX, EPUB, Markdown and TXT files, article links and text scanned from images.
  • No audio export from ElevenReader itself. ElevenLabs says export happens on its web platform or in the ElevenLabs app.

Should you use ElevenReader or LoudScript on Mac?

Choose ElevenReader when

  • You want ElevenReader’s voices and already listen on your phone.
  • You read EPUB books or import articles by link.
  • You are fine working in a browser tab.

Choose LoudScript when

  • You want to hear text from any Mac app without copying it into a browser.
  • You want speech that keeps working without an internet connection and without a monthly limit.
  • You want the current sentence highlighted in the original PDF.
  • You want a floating player above other windows and a searchable reading history.

You can use both. The Mac text-to-speech comparison covers six more options.

What stays on your Mac with LoudScript?

Apple voices and LoudScript’s downloaded speech models synthesize on your Mac. Screenshot OCR uses Apple’s on-device Vision framework. LoudScript does not send raw speech text, OCR output or source-app identity to product analytics.

Optional AI formatting is the exception. It is off until you turn it on. When it is on, the text being formatted goes through LoudScript’s server to a third-party AI model provider. Read the privacy policy before you enable it.

Try LoudScript on your Mac

LoudScript is a free, signed and notarized download for macOS 15.6 or later. It supports Intel and Apple silicon Macs, although Kokoro and Phonon require Apple silicon.
