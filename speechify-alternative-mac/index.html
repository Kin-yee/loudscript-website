LoudScript / Speechify alternative

Is there a free Speechify alternative for Mac?

Yes. LoudScript is a free native Mac app that reads selected text in any app, text in screenshots and PDF pages. Its voices run on your Mac, and it needs no account or subscription. Speechify still fits better if you want its large voice catalog, AI summaries or one library across your phone and computer.

How do you switch from Speechify to LoudScript?

  1. Download LoudScript from the download page and move it to Applications.
  2. Pick a voice in the welcome flow. You can hear it before you download it.
  3. Turn on selected-text reading when LoudScript asks for Accessibility access.
  4. Select text in any app and press Option-Command-R. This replaces Speechify’s Option-A. You can change the shortcut in Settings.
  5. Open a PDF in LoudScript when you want to follow the highlighted sentence in the original page.

What do you give up by leaving Speechify?

  • A library that follows you to iPhone, Android, Windows and the web.
  • Speechify’s Premium voice catalog and its AI summaries and chats.
  • Speechify’s voice typing.
  • Premium speeds up to 5x. LoudScript plays up to 2x.

LoudScript also does not import EPUB books or web page links. If you need those, look at the options below.

What other Speechify alternatives work on Mac?

  • macOS Read & Speak is built in and free. Turn on Speak selection and press Option-Esc.
  • Sandbook is free with no in-app purchases. It reads EPUB and PDF files and needs an Apple silicon Mac.
  • Aloud is a native menu bar app for $19 per Mac. It exports M4A.
  • NaturalReader has a free plan in the browser with unlimited listening on its free voices.

What stays on your Mac with LoudScript?

Apple voices and LoudScript’s downloaded speech models synthesize on your Mac. Screenshot OCR uses Apple’s on-device Vision framework. LoudScript does not send raw speech text, OCR output or source-app identity to product analytics.

Optional AI formatting is the exception. It is off until you turn it on. When it is on, the text being formatted goes through LoudScript’s server to a third-party AI model provider. Read the privacy policy before you enable it.

Try LoudScript on your Mac

LoudScript is a free, signed and notarized download for macOS 15.6 or later. It supports Intel and Apple silicon Macs, although Kokoro and Phonon require Apple silicon.
