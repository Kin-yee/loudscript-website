How do I turn on AI formatting?

AI formatting fixes typos, doubled words, missing full stops, and footnote markers before LoudScript reads text aloud. It is off by default.

  1. Open Settings (⌘ ,).
  2. Click Playback.
  3. Turn on Use AI Formatting Before Speaking.

How do I download a speech model?

Apple System voices are built in. Other models download once, then work offline.

  1. Open Settings › Models.
  2. Click Download next to a model.
  3. Click Use next to the model, unless it already shows Active.

How do I change a voice or remove a model?

Change the voice

  1. Open Settings › Models.
  2. Click Model Details under the model.
  3. Choose a voice from the Voice menu.

Remove a model

  1. Open Settings › Models.
  2. Click ⋯ next to the model, then Remove….
  3. Click Remove.

How do I read selected text in other apps?

Select text, then press ⌥ ⌘ R. Or Control-click it and choose Services › Read with LoudScript.

To allow the shortcut:

  1. Open Settings › Permissions.
  2. Click Open Accessibility Settings.
  3. Turn on LoudScript.

How do I read text on screen?

Press ⌥ ⌘ R with nothing selected, then drag over the text.

To allow screen capture:

  1. Open Settings › Permissions.
  2. Click Open Screen Recording Settings.
  3. Turn on LoudScript, then reopen LoudScript if macOS asks.

What do the keyboard shortcuts do?

  • ⌥ ⌘ R reads the selected text, or lets you drag over text on screen.
  • ⌥ ⌘ P pauses or resumes. With nothing playing, it works like ⌥ ⌘ R.
  • Esc stops reading while the floating player is open.

Change them in Settings › Shortcuts.
