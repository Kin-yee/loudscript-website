How do I turn on AI formatting?

AI formatting is off when you install LoudScript. Turn it on, and LoudScript tidies text before it reads it aloud. It fixes clear typos, doubled words, and missing full stops, and it removes footnote markers such as [1]. It does not change your wording.

  1. Open Settings. Choose LoudScript › Settings, or press ⌘ ,.
  2. Click Playback in the toolbar at the top of the Settings window.
  3. Scroll down to Read Aloud. In the AI Formatting card, turn on Use AI Formatting Before Speaking.

What changes when AI formatting is on

While LoudScript formats the text, the player shows Formatting. Then it starts reading. Each formatted read gets a Formatted | Original switch above the text. Click Original to see the text exactly as you gave it. Switching while LoudScript is reading stops playback.

What to know before you turn it on

  • It needs an internet connection. LoudScript sends the text to its server, which passes it to an AI model provider. The app says: “Your text is sent to our server for formatting and is never stored.” See the privacy policy for details.
  • Reading can start a moment later, because formatting happens first.
  • If formatting fails, LoudScript reads the original text. This happens when you are offline, when the server is busy or returns an error, or when it does not answer within 12 seconds. In a very long text, some parts may be read unformatted.
  • Some text is never formatted. LoudScript skips text with fewer than three words and text that looks like code.
  • It applies to new reads. That includes text you type or paste, the Services menu, the Read Shortcut, and screen captures. Replaying a read from History does not format it again.
  • Imported PDFs are not formatted. To format text from a PDF, copy it and paste it into LoudScript.
  • LoudScript always joins words split across lines. A word that a PDF broke with a hyphen at the end of a line is read as one word, with AI formatting on or off.
  • It needs no account. There is nothing to sign in to or buy.

How do I download a speech model?

Apple System voices are built into macOS and need no download. Supertonic, Kokoro, PocketTTS, and Phonon are speech models that you download once. They then run on your Mac without an internet connection.

  1. Open Settings › Models. Or click the model name in the player bar and choose Manage Models….
  2. Click Download next to the model you want. The button shows the download size.
  3. Wait for the progress bar to finish. Click Cancel to stop the download.

If Apple System is your current model and nothing is playing, LoudScript switches to the new model when the download finishes. Otherwise, click Use next to the model.

Until Supertonic is downloaded, the bottom of the History sidebar shows an Install offline voice card. Click it to download Supertonic, or close it.

How do I switch speech models?

Click the model name in the player bar at the bottom of the main window, then choose a model. The list shows Apple System and the models you have downloaded.

You can also switch models in these places:

  • The menu bar, while the main window is in front: Playback › Speech Model.
  • Settings › Models: click Use next to a downloaded model. The current model shows Active.
  • Settings › Playback: the Speech Model menu.

Switching models while LoudScript is reading stops playback. Press play to start again with the new model.

How do I change a voice or remove a model?

Each model keeps its own voice. Both tasks are in Settings › Models.

Change the voice

  1. Open Settings › Models.
  2. Click Model Details under the model. It is already open for the active model.
  3. Choose a voice from the Voice menu.

Kokoro and PocketTTS also have a Language menu. Changing the language may download a model pack. Kokoro downloads a new voice the first time you use it. For Apple System, Manage Voices… opens the macOS voice settings, where you can download more Apple voices.

Remove a model

  1. Open Settings › Models. The heading shows how much space your downloaded models use.
  2. Click the ⋯ button next to the model, then choose Remove….
  3. Click Remove to confirm.

If you remove the active model, LoudScript uses Apple System instead. You can download the model again later. Apple System is part of macOS and cannot be removed.

How do I let LoudScript read selected text in other apps?

Select text in any app, then use one of these:

  • The Services menu. Control-click the selection and choose Services › Read with LoudScript. This needs no extra permission.
  • The Read Shortcut, ⌥ ⌘ R by default. This needs Selected Text access, which uses macOS Accessibility permission.

To allow Selected Text access:

  1. Open Settings › Permissions.
  2. Click Open Accessibility Settings if Selected Text Access shows Not Granted.
  3. Turn on LoudScript in the list in System Settings, then go back to LoudScript.

The selected text guide covers both methods in more detail.

How do I read text from the screen or an image?

Press the Read Shortcut with nothing selected. Drag over the text on screen. LoudScript recognizes the text on your Mac and reads it.

This needs Screen Recording permission. macOS asks for it the first time. If you said no, LoudScript shows a Screen Recording Required alert. Click Open Settings in the alert, or open Settings › Permissions and click Open Screen Recording Settings next to Onscreen Text Access. Turn on LoudScript. If macOS asks you to quit and reopen LoudScript, do that, then try again.

The screenshot OCR guide explains what LoudScript captures and keeps.

How do I change the keyboard shortcuts?

  1. Open Settings › Shortcuts.
  2. Click the shortcut you want to change. The button changes to “Press keys”.
  3. Press the new key combination. It must include ⌘, ⌥, or ⌃. Press Esc to cancel.
  • Read Shortcut, ⌥ ⌘ R, reads the current selection from any app.
  • Play/Pause Shortcut, ⌥ ⌘ P, pauses or resumes speech. When nothing is playing, it works like the Read Shortcut. It reads the selected text, or lets you drag over text on screen.
  • Esc stops speech while the floating player is on screen. You cannot change this key.

If macOS cannot register a shortcut, another app may already use it. Choose a different one. Restore Defaults resets both shortcuts.

How do I change the reading speed?

Click the speed button in the player bar. It shows the current speed, such as 1x. Drag the slider. You can also change it in Settings › Playback › Speaking Rate. Both controls change the same setting.

The speed goes from 0.5x to 2x in steps of 0.05. Audio you export uses the same speed.

How do I open LoudScript at login or change its language?

Both settings are in Settings › General.

  • Launch at login is off by default. Turn it on to open LoudScript when you sign in to your Mac.
  • Application Language follows your Mac by default. LoudScript is available in English, Spanish, German, French, Dutch, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese. After you choose a language, click Relaunch Now.
