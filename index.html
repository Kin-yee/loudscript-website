Frequently asked questions

LoudScript for Mac FAQ

How do I make my Mac read selected text aloud?

Select text in any app and choose Read with LoudScript from the Services menu, or press Option-Command-R after enabling Selected Text access.

Can LoudScript read text from screenshots or images?

Yes. Capture an area of your screen and LoudScript will find and read the text. Screen Recording permission is required.

Can LoudScript read PDFs?

Yes. Import up to 50 pages at a time. LoudScript shows the original PDF beside the extracted text and highlights the current sentence.

Does LoudScript work offline?

Yes. Apple voices work on your Mac. Supertonic, Kokoro, PocketTTS, and Phonon also work locally after you download their models. When AI formatting is on, it sends the text through LoudScript's server to an AI model provider.

Can LoudScript save speech as an audio file?

Yes. LoudScript exports speech as a WAV file when you use Apple voices or a downloaded Supertonic model. Kokoro, PocketTTS, and Phonon do not export audio. LoudScript suggests a file name from the document, source title, or first sentence, then shows the saved file in Finder.

Which Macs support LoudScript?

LoudScript requires macOS 15.6 or later. Apple voices, Supertonic, and PocketTTS support Intel and Apple silicon Macs. Kokoro and Phonon require Apple silicon.
